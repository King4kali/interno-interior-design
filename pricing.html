Good to know

Questions before we begin.

How do we know which package is right?

Tell us the number of rooms, whether construction is involved and how much implementation support you want. We will recommend the smallest scope that can achieve the result well.

Are furniture and construction included in the fee?

No. Package prices cover Interno's design services. Furniture, materials, trades, specialist consultants, freight and taxes are budgeted separately and approved by you before purchase.

Can you work with clients outside New York?

Yes. Concept and signature projects can be delivered remotely. Full-service residences are considered by location, scope and the availability of a trusted local contractor.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

We review the project details and reply with availability within two business days. If the fit looks promising, we schedule a short discovery call before preparing a formal proposal.

Can the scope change after we begin?

Yes, with a written scope adjustment. We explain the effect on design fees and timing before any additional work starts, so there are no surprise invoices.
