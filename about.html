Our story

We begin by looking closer.

Interno began with a simple belief: a home becomes beautiful when it first becomes honest. We study the architecture, the rituals of daily life and the objects already carrying meaning before we draw a single line.

Independent residential studio founded in Brooklyn

Projects across New York, the East Coast and remotely

A close network of makers, artists and specialist trades

What guides us

Principles we bring to every room.

Our aesthetic changes with every client. These values do not.

01

Useful beauty

Every gesture must earn its place. We pursue visual clarity and everyday ease in equal measure.
